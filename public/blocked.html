Downloads paused

This is an automatic download limit, not a problem with the site. Your network address downloaded an unusually large number of documents in a short time, so document downloads from it are paused for a while.

Everything else on EPIC keeps working, including search and project pages. The Environmental Assessment Office uses this limit to keep the site fast for everyone.

An office or a VPN often gives many people the same network address. If several of them download a lot at once, they can reach the limit together.

How long it lasts

The pause usually ends on its own within 24 hours. If it lasts longer, contact an EPIC administrator.

Need many documents?

Contact an EPIC administrator and tell them the project and the documents you need.

Think this is a mistake?

Contact an EPIC administrator and include the date and time you saw this page.
